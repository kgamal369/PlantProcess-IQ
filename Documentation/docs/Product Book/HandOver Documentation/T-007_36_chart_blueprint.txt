THE ACCEPTANCE STANDARD, STATED ONCE. Every chart needs an intentional visual
story, sufficient population and cardinality, realistic variation, and at least
one meaningful drill or filter. NOT EVERY CHART MUST REVEAL A CORRELATION.
A hundred clean correlations makes a plant look fabricated, and that costs more
credibility than a flat chart does.

HOW TO READ THE COLUMNS
  MUST SHOW   what the chart exists to say.
  BORING IF   the specification. The data has to beat this. A chart whose
              boring condition is true on the day is a chart not to show.
  TODAY       what the MEASURED data supports right now:
                OK     - achievable on current data
                THIN   - achievable but the story will be weak
                BLOCKED- a required field or code does not exist
                UNVER  - depends on a column not yet read; see inventory sect. 9
