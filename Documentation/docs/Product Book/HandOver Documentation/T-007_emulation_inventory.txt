7. GENEALOGY AND CONSERVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

heat_no -> cast_pieces -> hsm_coils -> parsytec / pkl / QA, grade inherited.
Conservation is enforced in the generator rather than asserted in a document:
coil_width = slab_width - 2..6 mm; coil_weight = slab_weight * 0.985;
coil_length = slab_length * thickness_ratio * 0.985.
