TWO GAPS STATED RATHER THAN GUESSED.
  Downtime and yard carry no recorded row count in FLEET_RELATIONS.md. The
  document describes downtime semantics - equipment stoppage against production
  stoppage, buffer absorption, breakout cascades - but never states a count.
  Neither figure is invented here. Count them before the widget coverage matrix
  binds a chart to either.

ONE NAMING SURPRISE WORTH RECORDING.
  Downtime is staged under src_inspection_mysql_shape, not a downtime schema of
  its own, and QA results sit under src_pkl_mssql_shape. The staged schema names
  therefore do NOT map one-to-one onto the eight declared source systems. That is
  a staging-layout fact, not a defect, but a mapping written from the source list
  alone would be wrong.

what proves a configurable platform rather than one hardcoded schema.

  CONTROLS, which MUST NOT correlate:  SCRATCH 1.0x, DENT, SEAM - pure noise.

TWELVE OF SEVENTEEN HAVE NO RECORDED EFFECT SIZE. They are described but not
measured. Any chart that depends on R6 to R17 must have its effect measured
before it is placed, or the chart is a hope rather than a specification.

R4 AT 28.9x IS A RISK, NOT A PRIZE. A twenty-nine-fold effect is close to
deterministic, and a customer engineer may read it as planted rather than
discovered. R2 at 4.5x is the most believable of the five measured.

6. WHAT THIS INVENTORY MEANS FOR THE BLUEPRINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The emulated plant is close to complete. The gap is not the data.

All twenty-nine seeded widgets bind to EIGHT generic dimensions - day, week,
month, materialUnitType, defectType, severity, equipment, parameterCode - and SIX
generic measures - materialCount, observationCount, defectCount, defectRate,
riskScore, avgParameterValue.

NOT ONE widget touches grade, shift, crew, superheat, casting speed, finishing
temperature, chemistry, gauge, roll campaign age, tundish age, lubrication
viscosity, scrap ratio, coil position, or the downtime split.

Artifact 2 is the specification that closes that gap.
