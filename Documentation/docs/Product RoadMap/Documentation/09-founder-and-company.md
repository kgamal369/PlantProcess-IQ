## The company

SOU Industrial Software builds PlantProcess IQ, a read-only, evidence-grade process-to-quality intelligence platform for manufacturing plants. The company's focus is narrow and deliberate: connect the data a plant already produces, build one trustworthy picture of its material genealogy, and help its engineers investigate quality and downtime questions faster and with evidence -- without touching the systems that run the plant.

## Domain background

PlantProcess IQ is built by a founder with [[YEARS]] years of hands-on experience in steel-plant process and tracking systems, including work on [[BACKGROUND_DETAIL -- e.g. Level 2 tracking and process systems, and time at PSI Metals]]. The product reflects problems lived on real plant floors rather than a generic analytics template applied to manufacturing from the outside: the fragmentation of plant data across many systems, the difficulty of walking a finished product's genealogy back to its origin, and the cost of acting on a conclusion that the data does not actually support.

## Why this matters to an evaluation

A plant intelligence product is only as good as its understanding of how a plant's data actually behaves -- the mismatched keys between systems, the transition products that draw from two heats, the inspection devices that each describe defects their own way, and the records that quietly go missing. PlantProcess IQ is designed around those realities, and its honest framing of findings comes from understanding what plant-history analysis can and cannot prove.

## How we work with a first customer

The first engagement is a bounded, read-only pilot against your own data, with written success criteria agreed in advance and the pilot fee credited toward a subscription on conversion. The intent is to prove value on your data, on your question, with the lowest possible risk to your systems and your time.
