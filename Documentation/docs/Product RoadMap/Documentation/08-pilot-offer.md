## Purpose

This pilot is a time-boxed, low-risk engagement that proves PlantProcess IQ against your own plant data, on your own quality question, before any broader commitment. It is read-only throughout; nothing in your source or control systems is written to or changed.

## What the pilot delivers

- The connected sources mapped into one canonical genealogy on your own key names.
- A working investigation of the target question, with each finding showing its population, method, and exclusions.
- Dashboards and KPIs for the chosen area.
- A bounded value estimate for the target question, with every input drillable to its source.

## Success criteria

The pilot is considered successful when [[SUCCESS_CRITERIA -- e.g. the genealogy is walked end to end on the customer's keys, a defect investigation reproduces a known finding, and a value estimate is produced for the target question]]. These criteria are agreed in writing before the pilot begins.

## Why the risk is low

The engagement is read-only and bounded by source-load protection, so it cannot disturb production systems. It is time-boxed with written success criteria. The pilot fee is credited on conversion. And the product is honest by design: where data does not yet support a finding, it reports readiness rather than a fabricated result, so what you see at the end of the pilot is what the product genuinely establishes.
