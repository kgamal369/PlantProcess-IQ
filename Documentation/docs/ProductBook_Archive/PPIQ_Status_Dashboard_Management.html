08Milestone 2 — on-site delivery for soft testing

Decision required before commitment

Milestone 2 was scoped at 400 hours before it was redefined as an on-site installation. Derived bottom-up it is closer to 620.

The three largest gaps are not estimating noise. Practice learning, prediction and remediation were budgeted at 60 hours against a design specifying a fifteen-stage pipeline and a nine-check safety gate. Security, roles and licence were budgeted at 30 hours against one existing access policy covering 193 tables. The on-site package was budgeted at 40 hours including a tested backup restore rehearsal.

OptionShapeConsequence
AExtend M2 to about 620 hoursHonest — pushes the on-site date out by roughly 55 percent
BHold 400 hours as scopedThe compression lands on the two things M2 exists to deliver. Not recommended
CSplit: 400h Deployable Core, then 230h Intelligence CompletionRecommended. The on-site date holds

Why option C is right on engineering, not only on budget. During soft testing the customer's data is still accumulating, and the readiness gate requires 60 independent units and 40 outcome events before it will compute. Practice learning and prediction therefore cannot produce a real answer in the first weeks of a pilot whether or not they are built. Shipping them as a governed update partway through the pilot is better sequenced — and the customer sees no change, because those screens were frozen in M1.
