# PlantProcess IQ Air-Gapped Sizing Tiers

## Small smoke / pilot
- 2 vCPU API/Web
- 4 vCPU PostgreSQL
- 16 GB RAM total
- 200 GB SSD
- Suitable for demo, one plant area, limited history

## Medium plant
- 4-8 vCPU API/Web
- 8 vCPU PostgreSQL
- 32-64 GB RAM
- 1-2 TB SSD
- Suitable for multi-line production history and investigation dashboards

## Large enterprise / multi-site
- 3+ app nodes
- PostgreSQL primary + replica
- 128 GB+ RAM DB tier
- 4 TB+ SSD/NVMe
- Dedicated backup/object storage
