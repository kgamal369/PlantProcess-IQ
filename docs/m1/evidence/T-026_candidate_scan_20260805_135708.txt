==============================================================================
HOW THIS IS USED
==============================================================================
Three seed phenomena get authored from these numbers, not from expectation:
  a PASS  - a relationship the fleet genuinely carries, with a band set
            around the measured value rather than fitted to it
  a FAIL  - a natural predeclared expectation the data refutes
  a REFUSAL - a population that genuinely cannot support its claim, so the
            harness reports INSUFFICIENT instead of judging

A band written around a measured value is still an expectation: the harness
re-measures it after every fleet change, and a band that stops holding is
the signal. What must not happen is a band invented to guarantee a PASS.
